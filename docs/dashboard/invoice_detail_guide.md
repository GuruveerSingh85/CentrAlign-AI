# Invoice detail field guide

- **Document ID:** FIN-DASH-009
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Dashboard guide
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

The invoice detail page presents its source facts in sections: summary, line items and totals, vendor and PO checks, potential issue/next step, approval history, related records, internal demo notes, and review actions. `Vendor verification`, `Mock bank-details status`, `PO vendor match`, and `Receipt / service confirmation` are individually labeled fields.

The approval tier is calculated using the total USD amount. Approval history shows only seeded evidence; an empty history means no approval evidence is recorded. Related links open the vendor, PO, duplicate comparator, and knowledge articles. The local note form saves only to this browser's local storage.
