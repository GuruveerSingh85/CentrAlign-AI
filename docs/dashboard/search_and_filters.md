# Search and filters

- **Document ID:** FIN-DASH-008
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Dashboard guide
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

The top search accepts stable invoice, vendor, PO, approval, payment-run, or article IDs and article titles. Invoice list search also checks vendor name, supplier invoice number, PO identifier, and review flag; the adjacent status selector filters by the displayed review status.

The Knowledge Base search checks article title, category, and full Markdown body. The category selector filters article groups. Filters are local and deterministic; no results produces a visible empty-state message. Use exact IDs when multiple records have similar names.
