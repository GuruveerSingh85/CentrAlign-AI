# Payment runs page guide

- **Document ID:** FIN-DASH-006
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Dashboard guide
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

`/payment-runs` lists mock run ID, planned date, invoice count, total, currency, approval state, validation state, and status. `/payment-runs/:runId` shows included invoice records, computed total, excluded invoices and reasons, checks, and policy links.

Draft, Validation Required, Awaiting Approval, Approved for Demo, On Hold, and Simulated Complete are mock states only. Simulated Complete is reserved for a pre-seeded historical record. No control can transmit a payment or make a bank connection.
