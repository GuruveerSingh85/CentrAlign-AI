The Overview route is `/dashboard`. It shows the fixed demo date, summary counts, a Priority work queue, Needs attention links, and recent simulated activity. The metrics are calculated from the seeded local records and are intended for navigation, not financial reporting.

Queue entries link to invoice details. Attention links lead to the invoice or vendor containing the exception. Recent activity is historical mock data; a line mentioning a settlement always refers to a clearly labeled seeded historical mock record.

Use the queue to find candidate work, then open the source record and linked vendor, PO, approval, and policy pages. The overview summary is not a substitute for detail evidence.
