Ledgerly Financial Services is a fictional mid-sized business services company. Finance Operations supports purchasing teams by reviewing supplier invoices, checking vendor records, matching purchase orders, and preparing evidence for authorized approvers.

The Ledgerly Finance Hub is a synthetic browser-agent test site. All records, events, people, and amounts are invented. The portal has no bank connection, payment rail, email service, or real accounting integration.

## Invoice lifecycle

An invoice is received, checked for completeness, matched to a vendor and purchase order, compared with receipt or service evidence, reviewed for duplicate and exception indicators, and routed to the required human approver. An approved invoice may later be considered for a proposed payment run. Scheduling or approval does not mean that a payment settled.

## Important distinctions

- **Review:** compare records and document evidence.
- **Recommendation:** suggest a next step to a human approver.
- **Approval:** a decision by the authorized independent approver recorded in the approval record.
- **Scheduling:** placing an eligible invoice in a proposed batch.
- **Payment:** an external transfer. This demo cannot make one.

## Teams

Finance Operations works with requesting departments, Vendor Management, Finance Managers, Controllers, and the CFO. The Associate gathers and reports evidence; role authority remains with the named human approvers.
