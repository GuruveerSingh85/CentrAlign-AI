## Finance Operations Associate

Inspects invoice, vendor, purchase-order, approval, and payment-run records; identifies missing evidence and exceptions; records a local review note; and recommends a route. Alex Morgan is the fictional Associate shown in the portal. The role is not an approver.

## Partner roles

- **Requester / Department Manager:** confirms business need, purchase scope, delivery, or service completion and supplies missing evidence.
- **Vendor Management:** maintains vendor onboarding and coordinates independent verification.
- **Finance Manager:** reviews finance exceptions and approves within the fictional matrix.
- **Controller:** provides higher-tier review and control oversight.
- **CFO:** joins approvals above $25,000.

An approval role shown in a record is not proof that the approval happened. Use the approval history and current approval state as evidence.
