# Finance glossary

- **Document ID:** FIN-REF-001
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Reference
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

- **Invoice:** A supplier request for payment; it is not evidence that payment occurred.
- **Purchase order (PO):** A record of authorized scope, value, requester, and expected receiving evidence.
- **Receipt / service confirmation:** Evidence that goods arrived or contracted services were completed.
- **Duplicate indicator:** A similarity that requires investigation; it is not proof by itself.
- **Approval tier:** The human roles whose recorded approvals are required for an invoice amount.
- **Payment run:** A proposed group of invoices for later processing. This demo only displays a mock batch.
- **Payment status:** Whether an invoice is unscheduled, included in a draft/active run, or has a seeded historical mock payment record.
- **Vendor verification:** The mock record's state. It does not represent a real-world identity or bank check.
- **Exception:** A missing, conflicting, suspicious, or policy-blocking item requiring more evidence, a hold, or escalation.
