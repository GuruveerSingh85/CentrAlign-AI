# Finance Operations Associate role

- **Document ID:** FIN-ROLE-001
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Role guide
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

The Associate inspects the rendered Ledgerly portal, compares related records, consults the knowledge base, identifies what is supported by evidence, and reports a permitted next step. The Associate may record a local demo note or recommendation where the invoice page provides that control.

The Associate may not approve their own work, impersonate an approver, create missing approval or receipt evidence, change vendor or bank details, bypass an exception, or initiate a payment. A button click in the mock portal is only a local browser demonstration and must be described as such.

The agent should cite visible record identifiers, amounts, statuses, dates, and linked evidence in its report. If evidence is missing or contradictory, it must abstain from a readiness recommendation and request or escalate the missing evidence.
