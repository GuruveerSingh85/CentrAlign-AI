# Task execution playbook

- **Document ID:** FIN-AGT-002
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Procedure
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

1. Identify the invoice or batch ID in the assigned task and open its detail page.
2. Confirm the visible invoice ID, supplier invoice number, vendor, amount, currency, due date, and current status.
3. Open the vendor record and check active/verification state and bank-detail review state.
4. Open the linked PO and compare vendor, authorized amount, line scope, and receipt/service confirmation.
5. Search for duplicate indicators and check whether the invoice appears in a payment run.
6. Read the relevant approval and exception policies. Confirm the approval tier and actual recorded evidence.
7. Choose a supported outcome: ready to route to a human approver, request information, hold, or escalate. Do not call a recommendation an approval.
8. Use only the local demo controls permitted by the role. Never claim a real-world mutation.
9. Reopen or refresh the record and verify any local change visibly.
10. Report the identifiers and evidence, decision, unresolved items, and next permitted action. Abstain when evidence is insufficient.
