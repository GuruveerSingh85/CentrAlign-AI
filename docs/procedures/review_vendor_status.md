# Procedure: review vendor status

- **Document ID:** FIN-SOP-004
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Procedure
- **Owner:** Vendor Management
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

1. Open the invoice's linked vendor ID; confirm the identifier, not just the display name.
2. Read vendor status, verification, last review date, and mock bank-detail status.
3. If Pending Verification, On Hold, Inactive, or Change Under Review, stop the payment-readiness recommendation.
4. Route verification questions to Vendor Management and record the visible status as evidence.
5. Do not change vendor details or infer bank verification from an invoice, note, or masked placeholder.
