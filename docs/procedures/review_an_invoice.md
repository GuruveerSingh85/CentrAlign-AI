# Procedure: review an invoice

- **Document ID:** FIN-SOP-001
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Procedure
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

1. Search the invoice ID and open its detail route.
2. Check invoice number, vendor, dates, currency, amount, line totals, review status, and payment status.
3. Open the vendor. Confirm Active and Verified state and no bank-detail change under review.
4. Open the linked PO. Compare vendor, scope, amount, and receipt/service status.
5. Search for duplicates and inspect payment-run membership.
6. Determine the matrix tier and inspect approval history.
7. Record a local note with evidence. If evidence is complete, recommend routing to the correct human approver; otherwise request information, hold, or escalate.
8. Reopen the invoice and confirm any local demo state. State clearly that no real ledger was updated.
