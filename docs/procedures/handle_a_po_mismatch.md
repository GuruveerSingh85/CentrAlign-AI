# Procedure: handle a PO mismatch

- **Document ID:** FIN-SOP-003
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Procedure
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

1. Open the invoice and linked PO; confirm both IDs and the vendor identity.
2. Compare invoice lines and quantities to authorized PO scope and receiving evidence.
3. Recalculate invoice total and compare it with the PO amount. Apply tolerance only when the PO explicitly records it.
4. Treat missing PO, amount overrun, scope mismatch, and incomplete receipt as exceptions.
5. Keep the invoice on hold or request evidence from the requester/Department Manager; escalate material or conflicting facts to Finance Manager.
6. Do not edit the PO, invent receiving evidence, or recommend approval until the authoritative source resolves the mismatch.
