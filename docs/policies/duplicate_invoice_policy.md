A duplicate indicator is a reason to investigate, not proof. Compare supplier invoice number, vendor, amount, invoice date, service period, line items, PO, and any active payment-run membership.

Indicators include the same vendor and invoice number, same vendor/amount/date, small formatting differences in invoice number, or the same source invoice included in multiple runs. Different internal invoice IDs do not establish that two records are different obligations.

For the seeded `INV-2054`, compare the same supplier invoice number `MF-7781` and amount with historical mock `INV-2039`. Keep the new record on hold until a human resolves whether it is a resubmission or duplicate. Do not mark it safe solely because identifiers differ. Escalate an unresolved match to the Finance Manager.
