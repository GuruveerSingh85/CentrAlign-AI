# Payment-run policy

- **Document ID:** FIN-POL-006
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Policy
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

Before recommending that an invoice be included in a proposed run, confirm all required human approvals; active, verified vendor status; completed PO/receipt checks; no unresolved duplicate or other hold; no previous payment; no duplicate membership in another active run; and a sensible due date and terms. The run itself also needs its own required authorization.

The Associate can inspect a batch and report exceptions. They cannot execute, transmit, or settle it. `PAY-0087` is a draft awaiting review; `PAY-0088` is on hold because required invoice approval is incomplete. `PAY-0086` is a pre-seeded historical mock only. Never describe any batch as paid based solely on a draft or approval status.
