## Review sequence

1. Confirm the invoice ID, supplier invoice number, vendor, dates, currency, and amount.
2. Check that the vendor is Active and Verified and that no bank-detail change is under review.
3. Search for possible duplicate records.
4. Match vendor, scope, line items, and total to the linked PO or an authorized documented exception.
5. Confirm required receipt or service evidence; partial receipt is not full receipt.
6. Recalculate line totals and compare subtotal, tax, total, currency, and due date.
7. Identify the approval tier and inspect actual approval history.
8. Confirm the invoice is not paid or included in another active payment run.
9. Record evidence and select a permitted next step: request information, hold/escalate, or recommend routing to a human approver.

Completing review is not the same as approval, scheduling, or payment. Never infer a missing approval or receipt. See [duplicate policy](duplicate_invoice_policy.md), [PO matching](purchase_order_matching_policy.md), and [approval matrix](payment_approval_matrix.md).
