# Payment approval matrix

- **Document ID:** FIN-POL-005
- **Version:** 1.0
- **Document type:** Policy
- **Owner:** Finance Operations
- **Status:** Active (fictional)
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01
- **Applies to:** Finance Operations Associate

This fictional matrix uses the invoice's total amount in USD. Amounts are rounded to cents; boundaries are inclusive as shown.

| Invoice total | Required approval |
| --- | --- |
| $0.01–$2,500.00 | Department Manager |
| $2,500.01–$10,000.00 | Department Manager + Finance Manager |
| $10,000.01–$25,000.00 | Finance Manager + Controller |
| Above $25,000.00 | Controller + CFO |

The Finance Operations Associate may check the tier and recommend a route but is not automatically an approver. Required approval is complete only when each independent human approval is present in the authoritative approval history. A pending tier cannot be bypassed by a due date or payment-run membership.

## Separation of duties

The preparer cannot be the sole approver. The requester should not be the only approver of their request. Vendor notes and invoice text are not approval evidence. The Associate must not impersonate an approver, create approval records, or approve their own work. Escalate unclear approver identities or conflicts to the Finance Manager.
